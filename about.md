## Contributing

Contributions and text corrections are highly encouraged.

### Typos

If you find a typo in an article, feel free to make a pull request on our article repository [here](https://github.com/victorelgersma/oldnews-article-repo).

### Article Contributions

If you would like to contribute an article yourself, feel free to use [our OCR pipeline](https://github.com/victorelgersma/oldnews-OCR-pipeline) (MacOS + Linux only!), and submit your article as a pull request in [this repo](https://github.com/victorelgersma/oldnews-article-repo). Please e-mail your screenshots to [v.j.b.elgersma@students.uu.nl](mailto:v.j.b.elgersma@students.uu.nl).

If you would like to contribute but need a bit more technical support than the above paragraph feel free to e-mail me at the address above and I will happily on-board you into the process. Please note, however, that I cannot help you if you are a Windows user. 

## Source Verification

Every transcription hosted on this site maintains a direct link back to its material reference point. By utilizing the **'View Original'** button on any given entry, you can immediately review original photocopy clippings hosted by our image repository or visit their permanent records. 

## Source Websites

We are always adding new sources for our transcriptions. We are currently sourcing articles from the following sources:

- [The British Newspaper Archive](https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.co.uk/)
- [The Spectator Archive](https://archive.spectator.co.uk/)


## Scholarship

In addition to bringing the past to life for your amusement, we aim to facilitate original original scholarship. If you have written anything interesting based on our articles, we would be delighted if you let us know. 

So far, Victor Elgersma has based his mid-term paper on a few articles reviewer the infamous _Vestiges of the Natural History of Creation_. You can read his essay [here](https://samizdat.vjbe.net/2026-04-13-SciPub%20Research%20Essay%202-4.pdf)

Finally, the source code for this website is [also available](https://github.com/victorelgersma/old-news). Contributions are always welcome.
